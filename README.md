## Webby is BACK... again...
With a refreshed, sleek UI and more tabs, Webby has been remade.  
The **first** ever Tauri project by Lunar Team.

We welcome you to Webby v3.

## What's Different?

- **A Sleek, Refreshed UI**  
  We've completely redesigned Webby with a **sleek and modern interface**. Navigating through your tasks is now smoother and more intuitive, with a design that’s as stylish as it is functional.

- **Multi-Tab Experience**  
  Gone are the days of switching between screens. Webby v3 introduces **multi-tab functionality**, allowing you to easily jump between different areas of the app, enhancing your productivity and organization.

- **First Tauri Project by Lunar Team**  
  Webby v3 is proud to be the **first Tauri-based application** from Lunar Team. With this shift, you get faster load times, enhanced security, and a lighter, more responsive app than ever before.

- **Performance Boosts**  
  Every aspect of Webby has been fine-tuned for speed and performance. From **quicker startup times** to smoother transitions, Webby v3 delivers a more seamless experience across the board.

- **No more console app!**
  The biggest change. Webby originally started off as a console app coded in python. But now, it uses much more.
